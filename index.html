03 / UNDERSTAND

Built to be questioned.

The intended user is a nurse exploring how an AI model interprets an already completed cardiac checkup. The workflow makes uncertainty, missing values and model mistakes visible.

Several inputs require clinical testing, including exercise ECG, fluoroscopy and thallium testing. This is not a home screening tool or an emergency assessment.

Data & training

UCI Cleveland: 303 public records, 13 input features. Target: num = 0 versus num > 0. Stratified 80/20 split (242 training, 61 test). Median numeric and most-frequent categorical imputation, numeric standardization, one-hot categorical encoding, then logistic regression. All preprocessing is fitted only on training data.

Baseline & limitations

The baseline always predicts the most common training label. The historical cohort is small and selected, and does not represent a general population. Scores are not calibrated clinical probabilities. Coefficients describe associations rather than causes. No external-hospital validation was performed.

What we added

A nurse-oriented explanation workflow, missing-value transparency, an interactive threshold trade-off, sex and age subgroup evaluation, and real failure-case inspection. No real-person usability study has been completed.
